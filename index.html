Common questions

How do I find a code I made earlier?

Open History (the clock icon). Codes you saved, shared or copied are under Created, codes you scanned are under Scanned. Swipe right on an entry to mark it as a favorite.

"Join network" doesn't connect me to a scanned Wi-Fi

If joining fails, tap Copy password, open Settings › Wi-Fi, pick the network and paste the password.

My code won't scan

Keep a strong contrast between code and background (dark on light works best) and leave the white border around the code. For printed or scuffed codes, raise the error correction under Advanced.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
